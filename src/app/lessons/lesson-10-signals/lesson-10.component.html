Lesson 10 · Signals

Signals: `signal`, `computed`, `effect`

Signals are Angular's modern reactive state API. State is explicit, fast, and easy to reason about without manual subscriptions.

1. Core ideas

  • signal(value) creates writable reactive state.
  • computed(() => ...) derives read-only state.
  • effect(() => ...) runs side effects when dependencies change.

Interview takeaway

Signals remove a lot of RxJS boilerplate for local UI state. Use signals for component state, and keep RxJS for async streams like HTTP/websocket pipelines.
